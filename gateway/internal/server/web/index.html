How it works

  1. Proof of work. Your client burns about one second of CPU on a small puzzle. No email, no signup, no tracking — the puzzle is the gate.
  2. Token. The gateway hands back a short-lived anonymous token tied to nothing but that puzzle solution.
  3. Metered proxy. Requests are counted against fair-use limits and forwarded to DeepSeek on pooled keys.

Per-user daily limits

  • 20k output tokens
